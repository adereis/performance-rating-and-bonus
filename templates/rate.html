Rating Guidelines

Rating Scale
130-200% Exceptional — rare, sustained outstanding impact
110-130% Exceeded expectations
90-110% Met expectations — where most employees should fall
60-90% Needs improvement
0-60% Significant performance concerns
💡 100% is the baseline — a solid performer who met all expectations, not an "average" rating.
How Ratings Affect Bonuses
  • High performers receive exponentially more than their target (e.g., 120% rating → ~128% of bonus target)
  • Low performers are penalized more steeply (e.g., 80% rating → ~65% of bonus target)
  • The total bonus pool is fixed — giving one person more means others receive less
Required for Each Person
  • Performance Rating (0-200%)
  • Justification explaining the rating
  • 3 strength tenets and 2-3 improvement tenets
