📋 Calculation Methodology

  1. Total Pool: Sum of all bonus targets (from Workday, in your currency)
  2. Performance Multiplier: If rating < 100: (rating/100)^{{ params.downside_exponent }}, else (rating/100)^{{ params.upside_exponent }}
  3. Raw Share: Bonus Target × Perf Multiplier
  4. Normalization: Final bonus = Raw Share × Value per Share (where value per share ensures total = pool)

📖 For detailed explanation of the bonus algorithm, see Bonus Calculation Documentation on GitHub

ℹ️ Multi-Team Bonus Normalization

When calculating bonuses across multiple teams, normalization happens at the organization level. This means budget may shift between teams based on their performance distribution. Teams with concentrated high performers may receive additional budget from teams with more modest distributions.

📋 Understanding the Comparison

  • Team-Level Norm: Normalization factor if the team was calculated in isolation
  • Org-Level Norm: Actual normalization factor when all teams are combined
  • Budget Impact: Dollar amount gained or lost due to organization-level normalization
  • Teams with higher average ratings tend to gain budget, while teams with lower averages tend to contribute budget
