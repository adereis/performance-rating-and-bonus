Export Workflow Guide

Workday Copy-Paste Workflow
  • Click "Copy" next to each employee's description
  • Open the employee in Workday's bonus allocation screen
  • Paste into the Notes/Description field
  • Repeat for each employee, then submit the bonus batch
💡 The description includes your rating, justification, and bracketed data that this tool tracks.
How Bracketed Fields Work
  • [Strengths] and [Improvements] — Your tenet selections
  • [Mentor] and [Mentees] — Mentorship relationships
  • [YESAI] — AI-related activities flag

When you re-import from Workday, the tool parses these brackets to restore your data. Workday becomes the source of truth — avoid editing brackets manually.

Before Finalizing: Checklist
  • ✓ All employees have a Performance Rating (0-200%)
  • ✓ Each rating has a Justification explaining why
  • ✓ 3 strength tenets and 2-3 improvement tenets selected
  • ✓ Review the Dashboard for rating distribution sanity check
